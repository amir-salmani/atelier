# inspire — choosing a direction

**Read `../../method/inspire.md` for the procedure, then
`../../method/originality.md` for the gate.** Both are short and both are the
authority; this file does not restate them.

`teardown` writes facts. This makes choices. Do not merge them.

## Before you read either

1. **Journeys first, library second.** Opening the teardowns first produces a
   direction about the teardowns.
2. **Then the project's design system**, for the rules a direction has to
   survive.
3. **Then `grep` the library** for the mechanism you need. Do not re-read it.

## The two things that make it fail

- **A mood board.** Images without committed numbers cannot be built, reviewed
  or falsified. Three numbers are mandatory: tempo, easing, stagger.
- **A rigged ballot.** Three novel directions is not a choice. One of the three
  carries no new mechanism, and it wins more often than expected.

## Do not

- **Do not propose a direction sourced from one site.** Two unrelated teardowns,
  or one constraint of this product.
- **Do not drop a failed provenance row.** A table with no rejections was not
  audited.
- **Do not invent tokens.** Name what the design system will need to add.
- **Do not describe motion from a screenshot** — that is `teardown`'s job and it
  requires running it.
- **Do not compare a sampled duration with a declared one.** They are different
  measurements; `method/inspire.md` says which is which.

# Example journeys

**Not real journeys** — [`method/journeys.md`](../../method/journeys.md)
specifies seven sections, a named persona and an emotional arc, and these have
none of that. They exist so
[`evidence-lint`](../../method/evidence.md) has citations to resolve.

What they do demonstrate is the one rule the gate enforces: **every journey
names at least one `E-nn`**, or says plainly that there is no known pain.
